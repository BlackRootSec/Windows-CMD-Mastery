# Incident Response

**Source:** Volume 3, Part XXII; Chapter 50 (printed pp. 59–61).

The source covers what incident response is and is not, “do no harm” ground rules, evidence handling, where answers may be found, scoping and timeline building, containment/eradication/recovery order, verifying cleanup, and reporting.

Use the source's steps and the [lab report template](../../templates/lab-report-template.md). Keep collection actions proportionate, preserve original evidence where possible, record timestamps and decisions, and separate confirmed facts from hypotheses. Follow your organization's incident-response policy and escalation path.
