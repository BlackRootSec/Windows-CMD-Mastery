# Processes and Services

**Source:** Volume 2, Parts VIII–IX; Chapters 25–28 (printed pp. 37–52).

The source moves from process concepts and inspection to investigating a process, then services and service labs. When investigating, record evidence rather than jumping straight to a conclusion: process identity, parent/child context, path, and expected behavior all matter.

Treat changes to services or running processes as state-changing. Follow the lab's prerequisites and cleanup and avoid disabling security or system services to experiment.
