# Community Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants should help create a respectful, welcoming, learning-oriented community. Treat people with consideration regardless of experience level. Critique technical ideas rather than people, and explain disagreements constructively.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Welcome questions and corrections.
- Credit source material and contributors.
- Keep discussion focused on documentation and authorized, responsible use.
- Protect privacy: do not publish credentials, personal information, or private logs.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, insults, discriminatory conduct, deliberate misinformation, privacy violations, or using project discussions to promote unauthorized access or harmful activity are not acceptable.

## Maintenance

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that violate these expectations, and may restrict participation where needed. Report concerns privately through the repository maintainer's published contact method once one is configured. Do not post sensitive details in public issues.
